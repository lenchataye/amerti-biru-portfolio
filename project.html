01/06/2018

School and Community Sustainability Project

Together with my team members, I initiated a compost project at Astu Special School to address waste accumulation in our school and surrounding community. We created organic compost by recycling animal manure, fertile soil, ash, straw, and plant remains.

The project helps reduce environmental pollution, produces natural fertilizer for school gardening, and promotes awareness about waste management and sustainability.
